Moira is the MC of the story. She is one of the females of the main cast.


She seems to be a really good friend with Kiito.
She cant fly like some others of her friends, and because of that she prob feels a little excluded, but Kitto wants to help her so takes her to the air and they both fly together.

Moira seems to always wants to help other, even when she is afraid.
Maybe she puts other as the first priority even in front of her.
